## Decisions

| # | Topic | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | Instants, zones, and days | Instants are UTC whole seconds; sub-second input is truncated, never rounded up. Every entry stores its IANA zone (the request's `tz`, else the person's profile zone, else the workspace zone) and the local `work_date` it counts towards: the start's day in that zone for timed entries, the chosen date for duration-only entries. Instants must carry an explicit offset. |
| T2 | Durations and DST | A duration is the difference of UTC instants in seconds (`time/semantics.ts`), so a 23- or 25-hour local day counts the hours that passed. A timed entry's duration is the sum of its half-open segments; a deferred constraint trigger keeps them equal at commit. |
| T3 | Validity | Never negative; a manual entry lasts 1 second to `TIME_ENTRY_MAX_HOURS`; nothing ends more than five minutes in the future; a duration-only entry may not take a person's day past 24 hours. There is no backdating limit: only submission and approval close time (no silent locking rule). |
| T4 | No overlap | A person's segments never overlap (half-open intervals; an open segment runs to infinity; a zero-length segment covers nothing). The service checks first and returns `conflict` (`overlap`); a guard trigger under the person's transaction lock repeats it for every write path. Duration-only entries have no clock times and take no part. |
| T5 | Timers | One live timer per person (running or paused; unique index). Start, pause, resume, and stop are `{id}:{verb}` actions that are idempotent by key and by state: repeating one changes nothing and returns the entry. `replace: true` stops the current timer at the same instant. Clients may send the instant the person acted (`at`, up to `TIME_ENTRY_MAX_HOURS` late), so an offline pause or stop replays exactly. Elapsed time is computed from the server clock. A timer never exceeds `TIME_ENTRY_MAX_HOURS`: an action past it records exactly the limit. |
| T6 | Timer guard | The worker job `time.timer_guard` (every five minutes) stops timers whose total reaches `TIME_TIMER_MAX_HOURS` at exactly that total and marks them `auto_stopped` for review. Discovery is payload-free and bounded; each stop takes the person's lock before its rows (the API's order), so it never deadlocks with a person's own stop and never stops twice. |
| T7 | Writes | `time.log_own` (comment) on the work is decided at every write; only the owner changes an entry (others get `not_found`). Moving an entry to another task or project re-decides on the new target and checks both periods. Every write takes `timetrack.lock_person` first. |
| T8 | Reads and totals | Entries are readable through the work's compiled visibility (view; archived included; trashed and hidden never); guests read only their own. Lists, timesheets, approval views, reports, summaries, and exports apply that predicate in SQL, so hidden work is never named or counted. A person's own live timer stays visible without its target or note after they lose access, and can be paused, stopped, or discarded. Each entry reports `editable` from the same predicate at `comment` on active work. |
| T9 | Timesheets | A period is the seven days from the workspace week start (or the existing sheet containing the day). The owner submits a period (`timesheet.submit_own`; guests cannot): one `pending` line per brand with readable time that is not already approved, with totals and an entry hash; a live timer in the period must stop first. While submitted, all of the person's time in the period is closed for edits (any brand). Withdrawing returns undecided lines. |
| T10 | Approval | `timesheet.approve` (brand manage, decided now) per line, with `If-Match` on the sheet. The approver must read every entry in the line (`forbidden`, `incomplete_visibility`). Self-approval follows OD-20 (`self_approval_mode`; `auto` only with one active member) and is recorded and labelled. A decision writes immutable evidence (who, what, the entry hash and totals, the sheet version); approval also appends a `lock` row. A sheet stays submitted while any line is pending; then it is approved if every remaining line is, otherwise open. Rejection needs a note. |
| T11 | Locks and unlock (OD-26) | The latest `period_lock` row for a person, brand, and period decides. A locked period's entries cannot be created, changed, moved between brands (a task move is refused with `time_locked`), or deleted, by any path; the guard trigger repeats it. Corrections use the audited adjustment path: `timesheet.unlock` (workspace admins only, step-up, a reason) appends an `unlock` row and a security event, the line becomes `unlocked`, the person changes the time, and the line must be submitted and approved again. The approval stays as evidence. |
| T12 | Planned effort | Planned effort is the task estimate (TSK-023) and the project's time budget (`time_budget_minutes`), plus the sum of readable task estimates in the project; reports state their measure (`task_estimate`, or `task_estimate_by_due_date` for calendar periods). |
| T13 | Planned versus actual | `GET /time-report` groups by member (actual: the person; planned: the primary assignee), brand, project (a hidden project is never named; its tasks fall under no project), task, day, week, or month over a bounded range (`TIME_REPORT_RANGE_DAYS_MAX`). Planned counts readable tasks due in the range or with readable time in it. Rows are sorted by actual time and bounded (`TIME_REPORT_ROWS_MAX`, `truncated`); totals cover every row. Billable filters narrow actual time only. |
| T14 | Remaining effort | For a task: the remaining estimate, else the estimate less readable logged time (never negative), else unknown. For a project: the sum over its open readable tasks, with unestimated tasks counted; child projects are not rolled up (5C). |
| T15 | Export | `POST /time-export` returns CSV (RFC 4180, formula cells neutralized) or JSON of readable, stopped entries in a bounded range, up to `TIME_EXPORT_ROWS_MAX`; larger ranges are refused. Nothing is stored. Each export is audited (`time.exported`) with an `export` security event. |
| T16 | Rounding and display | Totals are sums of exact seconds; hours are rounded once, half-up, to 0.01 h; h:mm displays floor minutes. Notes and reasons never appear in events, metrics, or logs; audit stores their hashes. |

## Recorded deviations and clarifications

- **0B §6.6 shape.** Exactly one target per entry; `time_segment` and `timesheet_line` tables; `timesheet_id` and `locked` derived rather than stored; a per-person transaction lock and guard trigger instead of `FOR SHARE` on lock rows (ADR-27).
- **Purge.** Lifecycle purge of a task or project deletes its entries through the worker role (the only role the guard lets delete closed time); approval evidence keeps its totals and hash. A brand with approval or lock rows cannot be purged (the rows are R-EVIDENCE), which matches how brand purge blocks on other retained rows.
- **Week start changes.** An existing sheet keeps its period; a day inside an existing sheet always resolves to it.
- **Guests.** Guests with a comment share may log time on that work; they have no timesheets (`timesheet.submit_own` denies guests), so their time is never approved or locked.
- **Contributions.** The time-sheet view (VIEW-011) and the timesheet surfaces are the Time page's week and entries; 3B renders them through the query engine. Brand time investment (GOAL-011, RPT-011), the planned-versus-actual briefing (BRF-012), and the founder launch check (LA-013) consume `time-report` (brand and member groups) later.
- **Stored exports.** TIME-014 is a bounded direct export; scheduled, stored, and accounting-adapter exports are 6D.
- **Migration number.** `0031` is the next number after the Phase 2 head; 3A also adds `0031` in parallel, so whichever merges second renumbers its migration (the stacked merge order is recorded in the handoff).

## Consequences

Migration `0031_time_tracking` reverses exactly to 0030. The worker runs the timer guard. Tasks and projects gain a `log_time` capability and a Time panel (the project's Time section is live). `/time`, `/time/approvals`, and `/time/report` are routes.
