## Decisions

| # | Topic | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | What a notification is | One durable row per recipient and event (`notify.notification`, `ntf_`): type, record, actor, deduplication key, and small scalar facts (identifiers and counts, at most `NOTIFY_FACTS_MAX_BYTES`). It never stores a title, body, or address. The summary, record, brand, project, and actor are rendered when shown, from what the recipient can read at that moment. A row about something no longer readable shows only "You no longer have access to this item" with its time and state. |
| D2 | One pipeline, authorized twice | One durable outbox consumer (`notify.fanout`, with an inbox receipt, so at-least-once becomes exactly one effect) turns the canonical events of tasks, mentions, comments, reviews, access changes, reminders, and relationships into notifications. At creation each recipient must be an active member who can read the record now (security notices about access are the exception); at display and at send the same check runs again. Deadline notices (due soon and overdue) come from a scheduled sweep that fires once per task and due date. |
| D3 | Preferences and defaults | A person chooses immediate, digest, or muted per event type (or all), channel (or all), and scope (workspace, brand, project, or record), optionally ending. The most specific scope wins (record, project, brand, workspace), then an exact type over `*`, then an exact channel over `all`; brand and project defaults (`notifications.configure_scope`) rank below every personal choice, then the system default. System defaults are explicit and privacy-safe: in-app is always immediate; email is immediate only for what needs a person (mentions, assignments, review requests and decisions, failures, spend, permission and share changes, exports) and a digest for the rest; the browser is off until the person opts in and is immediate only for the urgent set. Permission and share notices cannot be muted. A mute scope the person cannot read is refused with the same issue as an unknown one. |
| D4 | Quiet hours and time zone | A person's time zone (their own, else the account's, else the workspace's) and one quiet window per day (which may cross midnight). Quiet hours hold non-security email and browser delivery until the window ends; they never hold the in-app centre or a permission or share notice. |
| D5 | Deduplication and grouping | Repeats of the same type about the same record by the same actor inside `NOTIFY_DEDUPE_WINDOW_MINUTES` collapse into one row with an occurrence count; new activity reopens a read or dismissed row and a saved row stays saved. An occasion (one reminder, one due date, one event) fires once. Grouping is a display choice for low-value types only (comments, due soon, overdue and similar); security and failure notices are never grouped away. |
| D6 | Digests | One cadence per person (off, daily, or weekly), so one digest carries each item. The slot is keyed by local date (or week) and is unique per person, so a worker retry or a second worker cannot assemble it twice; after an outage the latest slot is caught up and older ones are not sent late. Items are re-authorized at assembly, and again when the email is sent; hidden items are left out without a count. A digest holds at most `NOTIFY_DIGEST_ITEMS_MAX` items. An email past `NOTIFY_EMAIL_PER_HOUR_MAX` in an hour rolls into the next digest instead of being dropped. |
| D7 | Delivery and failure handling | A delivery (`notify.delivery`, `ndl_`) is pending, sending (leased), delivered, failed, dead, suppressed (with a reason), deferred for quiet hours, or expired. Delivered means the provider acknowledged the message (or the person's own browser reported showing it); queued is never delivered. An unconfigured provider suppresses with `provider_unconfigured`. Errors are classified transient, permanent, unconfigured, or rate limited; transient and rate-limited failures retry with exponential backoff (`NOTIFY_RETRY_BASE_SECONDS` doubling to `NOTIFY_RETRY_MAX_SECONDS`) up to `NOTIFY_DELIVERY_MAX_ATTEMPTS`, then dead-letter; permanent failures stop at once. A crashed sender's lease expires and the delivery is reclaimed under the same provider message id. Administrators see failed and dead deliveries (recipient, channel, class, code, attempts; never content) with `health.view` and retry them with `workspace.configure`, audited. |
| D8 | Browser channel | A provider-neutral boundary with no push service: the open page polls its own pending deliveries, shows them, and reports shown or declined; only that report marks the delivery delivered. A delivery nobody shows expires after `NOTIFY_BROWSER_ACK_TTL_MINUTES`. Permission is asked only after a user gesture, and the profile records granted, denied, or unknown. Lines are the minimal external form, re-authorized when fetched. |
| D9 | Privacy, retention, and erasure | External content is the standard line (actor and title) unless the brand is set to minimal or is sensitive, in which case it names only the brand and the kind (UXD-10). Failure messages, delivery history, events, and audit carry identifiers, classes, and codes, never titles or addresses. Notifications age out after `NOTIFY_RETENTION_DAYS` unless saved (OD-36 stays open: a technical bound, no published value); a purged record's notifications, deliveries, and scoped choices are removed with it; a departed person's notifications, deliveries, digests, preferences, and profile are removed by the personal-data eraser. |
| D10 | Contracts, boundaries, and readiness | Events `notify.notification.created` and `notify.delivery.state_changed` carry identifiers, types, channels, states, and classes. Sixteen named bounds (owner 4B), four signals, the `ndl` prefix, and the non-resource envelopes are registered in 0D. Readiness gains `email_delivery`: `ok` with a configured transport, `not_configured` (never `fail`) without one. 4B builds no document, file, or comment-in-document notices (4C, 4E), no collaborative editing (4D), no agent delivery (8B), and no external connector adapters; failure, spend, and briefing types exist in the vocabulary and arrive with the phases that produce them (6A, 7A, 7E, 8A, 8B). |

## Recorded deviations and clarifications

- **Provider-neutral email.** The email boundary is the existing mail transport (capture in development, none otherwise). No production provider is approved, so a workspace without one reports `not_configured` and every email delivery is suppressed with `provider_unconfigured`. This is the truthful readiness the brief asks for, not a failure.
- **Browser delivery without push.** No push service is approved; browser delivery is client-acknowledged while the application is open. Background push arrives only with a provider decision.
- **One digest cadence.** 0B's sketch allowed per-event digest choices with a separate daily and weekly flag; a single cadence per person keeps every item in exactly one digest.
- **Retention.** OD-36 (legal retention periods) stays open. `NOTIFY_RETENTION_DAYS` is an operational bound, not a published promise.

## Consequences

Migration `0037_notifications` reverses exactly to 0036. The shell bell, the notification centre, and the notification settings are real; My Work and the task and project pages are unchanged. The worker gains the fan-out consumer and one recurring delivery sweep per workspace.
